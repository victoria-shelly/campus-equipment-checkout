# IT Help Desk
This website is intended for students and faculty to create support tickets that include a category, description, and submission date. Students and faculty can
also view their current and previous tickets. Additionally, students and faculty may cancel their ticket if needed. Both users and IT staff may change their
website to be in light or dark mode. The IT employee can login, track ticket status, which has different priorities, assign the ticket to a different IT employee,
search or filter tickets, allow the IT employee to insert a resolution note before closing the ticket, and add due dates to the tickets. Finally, both IT employees
and users can log out of their account
